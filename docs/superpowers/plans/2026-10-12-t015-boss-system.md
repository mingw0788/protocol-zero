# T015 Boss System Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add the one-time 270-second mechanical tyrant encounter with readable charge telegraphs, real damage, and an independent health bar while preserving the shared combat pipeline.

**Architecture:** `BossSystem` owns one Boss entity and its approach/telegraph/charge/recovery state. The Boss stays in `GameWorld.enemies` so existing collision, weapon, damage, death, and enemy rendering systems can process it; `EnemySystem` skips Boss movement. GameWorld exposes an immutable Boss snapshot to Canvas and UI.

**Tech Stack:** Native JavaScript ES modules, Canvas 2D, DOM/CSS, Vitest, Vite; no new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-t015-boss-system-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Preserve SRS fixed Boss HP 1800, single phase, one spawn at effective time 270 seconds, charge warning of at least 0.7 seconds, zero experience drop, and an independent health bar.
- Preserve shared `Enemy` damage/kill settlement, D-009 death priority, and T016 ownership of victory/defeat/settlement.
- Keep all gameplay updates on the existing 1/60-second effective simulation; pause, LEVEL_UP, player-death freeze, reset, and no dependency installation remain in force.
- D-036 values are configurable first tuning values; do not present them as immutable balance commitments.

## Review Focus

1. **Threshold rounding:** 269.999 seconds remains absent; the fixed step whose endpoint reaches 270 spawns exactly one Boss. Cover in Tasks 1 and 3.
2. **Safe deterministic spawn:** choose the farthest legal outside-view corner when available; maintain full map bounds and player separation at all camera/map corners without consuming either random stream. Cover in Task 1.
3. **Attack timer boundaries:** repeated 1/60 steps produce a full 0.8-second telegraph, preserve the locked direction/endpoint, and do not move the Boss while warning. Cover in Task 2.
4. **World-edge charge:** clamp movement at the map edge without non-finite coordinates or extending the displayed warning endpoint. Cover in Task 2.
5. **Death/reset idempotence:** a defeated Boss is counted once, creates no crystal, never respawns in that run, and a reset permits one new Boss at 270 seconds. Cover in Task 3.
